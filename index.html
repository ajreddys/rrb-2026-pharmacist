About the exam

One CBT decides it

The Railway Recruitment Boards fill Pharmacist (Entry Grade) posts through a single-stage computer-based test (CBT), followed by document verification and a medical examination. Initial pay is ₹29,200 (Level 5, 7th CPC). RRB will announce the CBT dates on its website.

You can choose only one RRB.

Merit lists are drawn RRB-wise, and more than one application leads to rejection of all of them and debarment. Check your RRB's category-wise break-up in Annexure B of the notice before you apply.

Vacancies are provisional and may increase or decrease. Ex-servicemen and PwBD vacancies are counted within these figures.

Who can apply

  1. B.Pharm (Allopathic) from a Central or State Government recognised university or institute, or Pharm.D, and
  2. Registered as a Pharmacist under the Pharmacy Act, 1948.

You must hold the qualification by 14 Oct 2026; candidates awaiting final results are not eligible. This notice does not list D.Pharm. Medical standard C2 (distance vision 6/12, nil, with or without glasses).

Selection process

There is one written stage. Your CBT marks decide your rank within your RRB, and only as many candidates as there are vacancies are called for document verification, so clearing the qualifying mark is only the first goal.

  1. 1

    Computer-based test This course

    100 MCQs in 90 minutes (120 for eligible scribe users), ⅓ mark off per wrong answer. Qualifying: 40% UR/EWS, 30% OBC (NCL) and SC, 25% ST. Normalised if held in several shifts.

  2. 2

    Document verification (1:1)

    Shortlisted RRB-wise at one candidate per vacancy, on CBT merit. Ties go to the older candidate, then alphabetically by given name.

  3. 3

    Medical examination

    Medical standard C2, conducted by the Railway administration.

  4. 4

    Empanelment

    Railway zone or unit is allotted by merit, your stated preferences and the vacancy position.

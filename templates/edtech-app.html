Who can apply

  • B.Pharm (Allopathic) from a recognised university or institute, or Pharm.D
  • Registered as a Pharmacist under the Pharmacy Act, 1948
  • Qualification in hand by 14 Oct 2026 (awaiting results is not eligible)
  • Medical standard C2 (distance vision 6/12, nil, with or without glasses)

This notice does not list D.Pharm.

Exam pattern

The paper and the selection

100questions
90 min
  • Professional ability (pharmacy)70
  • General awareness10
  • Arithmetic and reasoning10
  • General science (Class 10)10

One mark each, four options, ⅓ mark off per wrong answer. RRB calls this split indicative.

  1. CBT

    Qualifying: 40% UR/EWS, 30% OBC and SC, 25% ST. Normalised across shifts.

  2. Document verification

    1 candidate per vacancy, RRB-wise, on CBT merit.

  3. Medical

    Standard C2, by the Railway administration.

  4. Empanelment

    Zone allotted by merit and your preferences.
